## Reduced-motion / anti-slop review notes

- The authenticated shell wraps page content in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` (`src/app/routes/AppLayout.tsx`). This audit is evidence only; no motion behavior was changed here.
- Several existing transitions animate layout properties (`height` or `width`); these are recorded as custom outliers and are not recipe candidates until separately redesigned.
- The 0.4–1.0s entrances and data-visualization draws are recorded as long/custom rather than silently normalized.
- Task 3.2 must centralize only opacity/transform micro-interactions and document any retained custom transition with its reason.
