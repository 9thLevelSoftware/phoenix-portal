How Form Analysis Works (No AI API needed)

  • Curve Consistency — compare force curve shapes across reps using normalized cross-correlation. High similarity = good motor pattern.
  • Tempo Control — measure eccentric vs concentric phase duration ratio from velocity data. Even ratio = controlled.
  • Fatigue Resistance — rate of force/velocity/ROM decay across the set. Slower decay = better endurance.
  • Bilateral Balance — L/R asymmetry trend across reps. Increasing under fatigue = compensation pattern.
  • Overall Form Score — weighted composite of all four metrics. Letter grade for quick glance.
  • Recommendations — rule-based: "if asymmetry > 10% on final rep AND increasing → suggest unilateral work"
